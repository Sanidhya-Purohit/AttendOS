> **The 75% Compliance Crisis:** Students currently rely on blind guessing and cluttered PDFs to track their attendance, leaving them highly vulnerable to subject debarment. AttendOS is an AI-powered engine designed to automate academic risk management and eliminate compliance anxiety.

<br>

### 🚀 Core Innovations

* **🧠 Visual Ingestion:** Uses Gemini AI to instantly parse messy timetable photos and PDFs, extracting course names, credits, and weekly slots to eliminate manual data entry.
* **🕹️ Interactive Bunk Simulator:** A real-time "Safe Bunk" sandbox that allows students to simulate missing classes and calculates the exact future impact on their attendance percentage.
* **⏱️ Dual-Timeline Analytics:** Instantly toggles the calculation engine between tracking the "Full Semester" baseline and a targeted "Till Next Exam" cutoff.
* **📈 Predictive Recovery Trajectories:** Advanced mathematical forecasting that calculates exactly how many consecutive classes a student must attend to recover from a critical deficit.
* **🔔 Smart Alarms & Copilot:** Context-aware notifications including 15-minute low-attendance warnings, morning first-class reminders, and a natural language chat.
* **🛡️ Emergency Safety Buffer:** A fully togglable setting that excludes a custom number of classes from calculations to protect against sickness and fests.
